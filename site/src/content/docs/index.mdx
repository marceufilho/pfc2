This one is certified: {right.proved} of {right.obligations} obligations proved, for every `lo` and `hi` the

## What you get

- **A language that is still C.** Contracts on the signature, invariants on the loop, arrays that carry their
  length, laws that are proved and never run. [Language](./docs/02-language/)
- **Proofs you did not have to write.** Counter bounds are inferred; the rest can be written, supplied as data
  or proposed by a language model, and is always checked. [Proofs](./docs/03-proofs/)
- **Whole programs.** Globals, strings, console input and output, `main`. [Programs](./docs/04-programs/)
- **A proof file, separate from the code,** that anyone can recompute offline.
- **Proofs as you type** in your editor. [Editor](./docs/11-editor/)

PFC2 is a research prototype. What it proves, what it trusts and what it has not been tested for are written
down in [Limits](./docs/09-limits/); how it relates to the thesis compiler it grew from is in
[PFC and PFC2](./docs/10-pfc-and-pfc2/).
